The homepage features digital artwork previews on Keepsake’s own “case-with-phone 15 pro.png” scan. The hero uses the actual photographed rim and camera surround. It does not imply the sample was physically printed. The original full-size scan stays outside the public repository.

The photo in assets/home/case-edit/ was rotated 90° clockwise, cropped around the handset and encoded as a 528 × 1078 WebP. The two SVG outlines in each slide are registered to that crop. The first clips away the scanner bed; the second keeps the design inside the rim and outside the camera. These are homepage presentation outlines only.

To change a featured design, use its original catalog artwork, export an optimized WebP, and update the slide image, name and customizer link together. Color, model, and artwork in the link should match the preview.

The Yosemite scene uses the National Park Service’s [Tunnel View photo by C. Jacoby](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/tunnel-view.htm). The two scene pickers link to the catalog colorway they display.

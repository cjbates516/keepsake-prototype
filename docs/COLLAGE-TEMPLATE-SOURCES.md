# Collage and words template art: sources and edits

All 18 layouts in `templates.json` (`tpl-*.jpg` art, `thumb-*.jpg` photo-free thumbnails) come from one purchased
template pack: **TemplateByCraft**. Corey added the first 10 in commit 6d10627 ("Collage v2: 10 TemplateByCraft designs
as native collage styles"). The 8 words templates (102, 68, 25, 60, 104, 72, 37, 87) come from the same pack.

## Licence: not yet confirmed

The pack's licence terms are not recorded in this repo and could not be found publicly. Before these templates are
sold on printed cases, Corey needs to confirm that the purchase licence allows printing on physical products for
sale, and record the licence text or a link to it here. If it does not cover printed goods, hold the templates.

Third-party material baked into the art and printed with every case includes: the blurred book, mug and sweater
photo behind the cards on 104, the window-blind light photo on 72, the handwritten letter scraps on 25 (legible
cursive sentences, kept on purpose for now), and the pressed flowers and line art on 37.

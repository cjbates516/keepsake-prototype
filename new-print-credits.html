Artwork & photograph credits

Space Photography uses actual telescope and spacecraft observations from the NASA image archive. Astronomical images may combine observations or map non-visible wavelengths to visible colors. Images are cropped and, for isolated subjects, placed on a black field for the case layout. No NASA sponsorship or endorsement is implied.

The new botanical, golf, liquid and park illustrations are original AI-assisted Keepsake artwork. Golf landscapes in this drop are fictional course illustrations, not surveyed maps or photographs of named courses. The existing Pebble Beach and St Andrews maps remain a separate collection.

Printing a case, start to finish

One job takes about 12 minutes of your attention and 8 to 10 minutes of machine time. The size table at the bottom is the law. When a step fights you, stop and read, do not wing it.

1Set the blank

  • Pull the blank named on the order card. Confirm the model on the inside of the package, not by eye.
  • Wipe the print side with a 70 percent alcohol wipe and let it flash dry.
  • Lay the case on the bed back up, camera end to the left, inside the printable area.
  • Once it is scanned, do not bump or move it. Moved case means rescan, no exceptions.

2Scan it

  • In xTool Studio press Scan, then Smart mask, method Subject, then Apply.
  • The scan should show the whole case with the camera bump clearly visible.

3Get the print file

  • Customer order: open the staff page, find the order card, press Download print file.
  • Shelf stock or demo: open the customizer, pick the phone model first, open the design, press Download print file (staff) under the case preview.
  • The file lands in Downloads and the name starts with print- and includes the model.
Or build it in Canva (custom photos)
  • Open Canva. Press Home, then Projects, then Your starred, and open Case Mask.
  • Pick the mask file for the case. The clear UV case file covers every model except the 17 Pro and 17 Pro Max, which have their own files.
  • Press Use brand template. Canva opens a fresh copy so the master file stays untouched.
  • Drag the customer photo in. It lands on top of the mask. Click the photo again and it snaps into the mask.
  • Press Share, then Download, turn transparency on, then press Download.
  • The file lands in Downloads or the folder you picked. Drag it into xTool Studio and carry on with step 4: same sizing rules, same 1 mm overhang.

4Import and size it

  • Press the insert icon (plus square, left toolbar), pick the file from Downloads.
  • When it asks about the working area choose Keep size. Never Scale to fit
  • Type the W value from the size table below into the W field. The lock between W and H must be closed so H follows. Confirm H matches the table.
  • Set rotation to 270 so the camera hole in the art sits over the camera bump. If it lands on the wrong end use 90 instead.
  • Slide the art over the scanned case. Color should hang about 1 mm past the case edge on every side, that overhang is the bleed and it belongs there. Arrow keys nudge in small steps.

6Print

  • Press Process, review the summary. Time should read 8 to 10 minutes. A wildly different number means the size is wrong, go back to step 4.
  • Press Print. Stay within earshot for the first minute.

7Inspect and log

  • Under the counter light check three things: the camera ring is clean, the color wraps the corners with no bare rim, and the colors are solid, not milky. Milky color means the white layer failed.
  • Photograph the case in the photo box.
  • Orders: tap Printed on the card. At pickup ring the custom print SKU and tap Picked Up.
Shortcut: Claude can run steps 3 through 6 for you. Scan the blank, then tell Claude the design and the phone model and watch it line everything up. A team member still inspects every print.

When it goes wrong

  • First failed print: check the size against the table, rescan the case, try once more.
  • Two failed prints on one order: stop and text Corey.
  • Banding or missing lines: run a nozzle check before the next job.

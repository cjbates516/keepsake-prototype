Photographs & original artwork

Ocean, rain, seasonal and golf photographs are sourced photographs. Botanical blueprints are historic cyanotype reproductions. The new tarot designs and two vintage golf illustrations are original AI-assisted artwork. Case previews are digital composites.

Photographs are resized without upscaling and cropped by each case’s print mask. Historic scan borders may be trimmed. ShareAlike adaptations retain their source license; attribution is included with the staff artwork files.
